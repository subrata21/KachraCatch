Kachra Catch! Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026
Developer: Lambo
Contact: gamessubstrate@gmail.com

1. About the app

Kachra Catch! is an offline casual game for Android. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no servers.

2. Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal or device information. It does not use the internet and requests no device permissions.

3. Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the app saves a few things only on your device:

  • your high scores for Normal and Ek Chance modes
  • the number of games played and your selected game mode
  • whether you have finished the tutorial
  • your music and sound settings

We cannot see or access this information. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. If you use Android's backup feature, Android may include it in your own device backup, which is managed by Google and you.

4. Third-party services

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services.

5. Children

The game is family-friendly and collects no information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds anything that uses data (for example optional advertising), we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
